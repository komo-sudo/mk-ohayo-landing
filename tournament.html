Before you start

Questions people ask first.

Do I own real shares?

With perpetuals, no. Every leveraged trade on this page is a perpetual: a contract that follows the stock's price, long or short, without shareholder rights or dividends. Spot is the other kind of market on OHAYO. There is no leverage there. You buy the asset and hold it.

Where is my money held?

In an account created for you at sign-up that only you can move funds from. Trades settle on Hyperliquid's public order book. OHAYO does not hold your balance.

How do I add money?

You deposit USDC, a digital dollar. The app shows your deposit address and the steps. The minimum deposit is 5 USDC.

What can go wrong?

Leverage multiplies losses the same way it multiplies gains. If the price moves far enough against you, the position is liquidated and its margin is lost. Open positions also pay or receive a funding rate.

How often do tournaments open?

The plan is a new one every day once tournaments launch in December, some run by OHAYO and some by creators. Each tournament lists its tickers, prize pool and the traded volume you need to qualify.

Is TradeView copy trading?

No. It shows you other accounts' real trades. Nothing is placed for you, and every order is your own decision.
